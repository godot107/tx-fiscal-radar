What we collect, and why

Fiscal Note Desk answers questions about public Texas bills and fiscal notes. It is built to keep as little about you as it can. This page lists everything it keeps.

What we don't do

  • We don't analyze the content of your questions to guess your views, politics or mood.
  • We don't sell or share data. Usage reports show totals, never individual people (a topic is only reported when at least five people asked about it).
  • We don't use your questions to train models.

Public records

If this service were run by a Texas state agency, records it keeps could be subject to the Texas Public Information Act. That's one more reason it keeps so little by default.

External AI tools (MCP)

If you connect your own AI assistant to the MCP endpoint, the same rules apply: each tool call is logged with a pseudonymous id and no query text, for 30 days. What your assistant does with the results is governed by its own provider's policy.
